## What would falsify the regime read

A hold accompanied by any sentence that conditions December on labor or housing, or a
presser that invokes long-end tightening as a substitute for hikes. Either would move the
intel descriptor toward "Potential pivot" and is the case the market is least positioned
for.

## Positioning note

calendar. The last time a boost carried into an FOMC day (2026-07-29, T2 at 2.0x) the day
closed -1.54%. The hold cohort is benign on average; the hike tail is not. Decision left
to the operator; the pre-registered expectation is that reducing to 1.0x before 14:00 ET
costs little in the base case and removes the tail.
